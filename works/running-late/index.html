Running Late

Running Late is a stop-motion animation which follows two monsters who are running late to see a movie.

Produced in under 12 hours for monthly short film festival Filmonik, I made Running Late to see how fast I could go from idea to creation to shooting. Using a minimal set and props I created the film the day it was presented.
